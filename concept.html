Executive Summary

The healthcare supply chain in Nepal has historically faced significant challenges, including fragmented equipment distribution, lack of transparent pricing, absence of verified second-hand trade, and uncoordinated repair services. Mediko Bazar is designed to address these critical inefficiencies by establishing a centralized, unified healthcare commerce platform specifically tailored for the Nepalese context.

The platform will act as an integrated digital bridge connecting clinical buyers (hospitals, clinics, diagnostic centers) directly with local vendors and international manufacturers. By incorporating rigorous administrative verifications, verified used-equipment criteria, structured rental custody rules, and directly mapped biomedical engineering support, Mediko Bazar delivers a comprehensive lifecycle solution for medical inventory management.

Our core objective is to replace manual, non-auditable communication channels with automated workflows. From initial import estimation and landing cost calculation to final quotation acceptance, tax invoice issuance, rental penalty processing, and warranty claim validation, the entire lifecycle is controlled under one secure, administrative console. This ensures complete transparency, compliance, and efficiency across all healthcare procurement and maintenance operations.

Business Model

B2B procurement, B2C purchasing, multi-vendor selling, international seller support, rental service, engineering service and used-equipment trading.

Core Value

A single platform to buy, rent, service, trade, import and manage medical equipment and spare parts with controlled approvals and support workflows.

Operational Control

Admin panel for product approvals, certificates, warranty, quotations, invoices, rental penalties, support tickets, chat and reporting.

Nepal Focus

Designed for Nepal delivery, Nepal landing cost calculation, vendor support and local healthcare procurement workflows.

Enquiry-to-Quotation Management

Every “Ask for Price” and sourcing request becomes an accountable workflow with ownership, response history, documents and conversion tracking.

Step 01: Submission
Buyer submits an enquiry for new/used equipment, spare parts, rentals, servicing, or customized landing cost estimates.
Step 02: Registration
System records request data, attaches documents, and issues a unique Enquiry ID, notifying admin/vendors.
Step 03: Assignment
System or support manager assigns ownership of the ticket to appropriate staff or registered vendors.
Step 04: Sourcing PO
International sellers supply base USD costs, lead times, and warranty boundaries when necessary.
Step 05: Calculation
Admin computes local duties, margins, and taxes to convert the import requests into localized NPR quotations.
Step 06: Issuance
Quotation is dispatched. Buyer acceptance triggers automated orders, tax invoices, and shipping cycles.

Enquiry Status Cycle: New → Assigned → Under Calculation → Seller Response Pending → Quotation Sent → Accepted / Converted / Archived.

Import Calculation & Nepal Landing Cost

Approved international sellers may display an export price in USD or another supported currency. That value is a sourcing reference; admin issues the final Nepal price only after cost and compliance review.

Landing Cost Formula: Product Cost (USD/Foreign Currency converted to NPR) + Freight + Insurance + Customs Duty + VAT + Clearance Costs + Bank/Forex Commission + Local Transport + Installation Charges + Mediko Bazar Commission + Margin + Contingency Reserves = Estimated Landing Cost.

Enquiry Inputs

Product dimension, total weight/volume, shipping mode (Air, Sea, Land), HS code, international origin, destination, installation needs.

Admin Rules Engine

Set up global parameters for freight per kg, customs rates based on HS codes, bank LC commissions, Ncell/local transportation rates, and contingency margins.

Price Shields

Keeps internal margins and seller raw prices hidden from buyers. Customer sees only the final, approved NPR localized quotation.

Commercial Output

Detailed breakdown for admins, summary breakdown for customers, exportable PDF quote, and direct transition to trading PO records.
